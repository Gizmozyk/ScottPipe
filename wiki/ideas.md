# Ideas

Features, forks, or patches worth looking at for ScottPipe.

- [malerva0/CloggedPipe](https://github.com/malerva0/CloggedPipe) — parental
  control: blocks playback of videos from channels the user isn't subscribed
  to, and requires a password to subscribe to a new channel. Interested in
  this functionality, not their implementation (old Java hack on a stale
  fork) — worth reimplementing natively against our current Kotlin/KMP
  codebase instead of porting their code. **In progress** — see
  [features/kid-mode.md](features/kid-mode.md) and
  [adr/0001-kid-mode-architecture.md](adr/0001-kid-mode-architecture.md).
